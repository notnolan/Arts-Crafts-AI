# Arts-Crafts-AI

A research project on the Arts and Crafts movement (c. 1850–1915), tested through close reading of primary texts. The notes are an Obsidian vault.

## Hypotheses

- **H1:** Craft in the Arts and Crafts movement was about personal development, meaning the maker's ability to develop a practice, and not only technical skill.
- **H2:** The movement distinguished craft at a technical level from craft at a theory- or practice-based level.

## Contents

- [Hypothesis Tracker.md](Hypothesis%20Tracker.md): definitions, metrics, evidence for and against, status.
- [Literature Review.md](Literature%20Review.md): candidate sources per hypothesis (Ruskin, Morris, Lethaby, Ashbee, Cobden-Sanderson, Wright, Crane, *The Craftsman*, and others) plus secondary scholarship.
- `Readings/`: texts being coded. Currently Ruskin's "The Nature of Gothic" and Wright's "The Art and Craft of the Machine."
- [References.md](References.md): source links.
